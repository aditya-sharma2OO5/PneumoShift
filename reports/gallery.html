Methodological Architecture & Statistical Rigor

The PneumoShift investigation conducts an exhaustive empirical audit of model generalization across in-domain (NIH) and out-of-domain (CheXpert) deployment regimes. Every reported metric is backed by:

  • Patient-Clustered Bootstrapping: 1,000 resamples stratified at the unique patient level (patient_id) to eliminate intra-patient correlation bias.
  • False Discovery Rate Control: Benjamini-Hochberg procedure with strict monotonicity and family-wise error protection (α = 0.05).
  • Multi-Dimensional Clinical Auditing: Simultaneous evaluation of discrimination (AUROC/AUPRC), calibration (ECE/Brier/NLL), decision utility (F1/Sens/Spec), and explainability faithfulness.
